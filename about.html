ABOUT 2ONE

A simple video shortener
for Shorts and short-form video.

2One is a free video length reducer for YouTube Shorts and short-form video. Make a video 59 seconds long, right in your browser.

How it works

Select a video and 2One looks for stretches where the picture stays still. It retimes those moments and the rest of the video to fit a 59-second timeline, while keeping the source frames. The video is processed in your browser and stays on your device.

The first time you use it, your browser downloads the video engine (about 31 MB). After that, choose your video, wait for processing to finish, then preview and download the result.

Made for quick, focused videos

The original idea came from screen recordings of puzzle games. Long thinking pauses made those recordings too slow to share as YouTube Shorts, so 2One was designed to compress the waiting time while keeping the sequence of frames intact.
